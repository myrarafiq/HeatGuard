Idea:
"same-day heat, safety planner for construction project managers"

Plan:
10-Day Build Plan

What we are building is a web application for a construction/HSE manager responsible for several sites.

Every morning it:

1. Knows the locations of the construction sites
2. Pulls the next 12 hours of hyperlocal environmental data from FortyGuard for each site
3. Converts those conditions into a heat-risk assessment
4. Considers what type of work is planned at each site
5. Shows when/where heat risk is highest
6. Recommends how today's outdoor work should be scheduled

7. Optional: uses an LLM to turn the calculated results into a simple supervisor briefing

---
